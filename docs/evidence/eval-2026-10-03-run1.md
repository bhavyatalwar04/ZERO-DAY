## Caveats

- **Labels** follow our operational definitions (ADR-006). Monitor (A, B, T) matches them by construction; system C is measured against them. This is agreement with the definitions, not ground truth about trader psychology.
- **The rubric uses lexicons:** it catches a missing pattern name or a contradicted price direction, not subtle wrongness. Grounding checks where numbers came from, not what they mean.
- **C can only quote numbers in its prompt.** It must compute percentages itself, and the grounding check rejects computed numbers it wasn't given. That is a real property of the one-prompt design (no verified computation step), not an artefact.
- **COV-20 prices are synthetic** (ADR-009). One run per system: LLM outputs vary between runs.
